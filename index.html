4. Industrial Logistics Dispatch Rules

The "1-Pallet & Over-Width" Trap

On a 2.40m intermodal tautliner or flatdeck trailer, pallets wider than 120cm cannot sit 2-abreast. If your cargo is 130cm wide and non-stackable, it consumes the entire 2.4m trailer width for its length. Even for 1 standard 1.2m × 1.0m pallet, if it cannot be stacked and no co-load partner exists, carriers bill for the full floor footprint.

The Rate Illusion Warning

A freight carrier offering R 2.60/kg on 1:250 terms frequently exceeds the invoice total of a transporter offering R 2.85/kg on standard 1:333 road terms. Always audit the chargeable mass before accepting a tender rate.
