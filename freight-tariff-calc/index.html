5. Industrial Logistics Dispatch Rules

The 2.40m Trailer Deck Floor Rule

On standard South African tautliners and intermodal trailers, the internal deck width is strictly 2.40m. If cargo is wider than 1.20m and non-stackable, it consumes the entire width of the trailer floor. The 2D visualizer above renders the exact physical footprint to prove where floor capacity was consumed.

The Volumetric Rate Illusion

A freight carrier offering R 2.60/kg on 1:250 terms frequently exceeds the invoice total of a transporter offering R 2.85/kg on standard 1:333 road terms. Always audit the chargeable mass before accepting a tender rate.
